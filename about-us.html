GRANDIX INTERNATIONAL

The Grandix International is a rich motivational tabloid published by the Stonepark Media. The magazine focuses on deserving individuals from all walks of life both in public and private organizations who have distinguished themselves in their various fields of endeavours. It showcases men and women who have made significant and remarkable impact to the political and socio-economic spheres of Nigeria. It also includes insightful features and interesting articles that will be of great benefit to you, your organization and household. Finance, health, relationship, entertainment and sports are also included.

THE GRANDIX INTERNATIONAL AWARDS FOR EXCELLENCE IN NATION BUILDING 2025

The Stonepark Media, publishers of Grandix International is pleased to inform you of its forth coming annual awards event taking place in the city of Abuja, the Federal Capital Territory in the month of December 2025.
