About Grandix International

The Grandix International is a rich motivational tabloid published by Stonepark Media. The magazine focuses on deserving individuals from all walks of life both in public and private organizations who have distinguished themselves in their various fields of endeavours. It showcases men and women who have made significant and remarkable impact to the political and socio-economic spheres of Nigeria.

Grandix includes insightful features and interesting articles on finance, health, relationships, entertainment and sports — content designed to inform, motivate and add value to readers.
